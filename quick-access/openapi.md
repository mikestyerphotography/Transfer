## College Audition Outfit/Clothing Advice

Thank you for reaching out! I’m happy to offer some recommendations. I actually went to school for musical theatre myself, so you’re in good hands. These days, the industry is more flexible with headshot attire, but I recommend keeping it simple while still incorporating a bit of personality. This is the only time in her career when she’ll be auditioning for the part of _herself_, if that makes sense.

Here are some tips to keep in mind:

* **Color**: Choose colors that complement her skin tone—consider pastels, earth tones, jewel tones, and neutrals. Blue tends to look great on most people, so I recommend bringing one or two blue options.
* **Layering**: Layering a jean jacket over different tops can add variety.
* **Jewelry**: Avoid necklaces, but stud or hoop earrings are fine.
* **Tops**: T-shirts, blouses, and long-sleeve shirts are all great options. I usually suggest avoiding tank tops unless she’s a dancer and wants to highlight that (just a personal preference).
* **Prints**: Prints are okay, but make sure they aren’t overly distracting, and avoid fine polka dots, which don’t photograph well. Small logos can be Photoshopped out, but avoid large text, logos, etc.
* **Personality**: The outfits should reflect who she is for her college auditions and the types of roles she wants to market herself for during regular auditions as well. We're aiming for a great everyday headshot that she can use for college/other auditions, and we should also photograph a more dramatic look as well as a brighter, more commercial-friendly look. Let me know if you have questions about this.
* **Number of options**: Bring at least five different outfit options for variety.
* **Bottoms**: No need to bring matching pants or shoes unless you're planning for 3/4 or full-body shots. If so, bring complete outfits.

This is a lot of information - feel free to check out my [actor headshots portfolio](https://mikestyerphotography.com/services/actors-headshots-and-branding) if it is helpful for you to see examples of what I am talking about. Let me know if you have any specific questions!

Warmly,

Mike
