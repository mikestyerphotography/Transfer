# Reschedule/Cancellation Policy

I understand that sometimes plans unexpectedly change, so I offer one complimentary reschedule per session if you give at least 48 hours' notice. For any additional reschedules, a $100 fee will apply.

If you need to reschedule with less than 48 hours' notice, it will be considered a cancellation, and no refund will be offered. I make exceptions on a case-by-case basis for things like medical emergencies.

If you need to reschedule, you will have 45 days to pick a new date and time or you will need to book another session.
