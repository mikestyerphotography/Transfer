# Lead/Shoot Naming Conventions

{% hint style="info" %}
**💡 Lead/Shoot names should always follow title case.**
{% endhint %}



## Studio Shoots



<details>

<summary>Actor Headshots, Business Headshots for Individuals*, Dating Profile Portraits, Personal Branding</summary>

{First} {Last} - {Shorthand of Shoot Type}\


_Possible Shoot Types:_\
_Actor, Business, Team, Booth, Event, SALT, Dating, ERAS, Theatre, Personal Branding, Business Branding, Model, or Actor and Business_\


{% hint style="info" %}
💡 **Examples:**  Mike Styer - Actor  /  Jane Doe - Personal Branding
{% endhint %}



</details>

## IPS Orders

### I make them a custom offer where if they purchase x number of images, they receive y number of images for free

Creating the order: Create a custom package. Add two line items - one for the number of images they paid for, another for the number of images I’m gifting them (gifted image).

**Naming convention:** CUSTOM | {Total Number of Images} Images - Buy {number of images paid for}, Get {number of images gifted} Free

_Example: CUSTOM | 29 Images - Buy 25, Get 4 Free_

### Exception: if I offer a certain number of images at a fixed price instead of offering them a certain number of images for free.

Create one line item with the service being Retouched Image and the quantity being the total number of images for the order.

**Naming convention for that:** CUSTOM - {Total number of images sold} Images

_Example: CUSTOM - 17 Images_
