# How someone can become a lead in Sprout

1. They fill out a contact form
2. We manually add them into Sprout
   1. They called us
   2. They texted us
   3. They directly emailed us
   4. They spoke with me in person
3.  They partially fully complete a booking page

    💡

    When someone proposes a date on the scheduler embedded in a booking page, they enter the system as a lead. They will be converted to a shoot once I countersign their contract in Sprout.
