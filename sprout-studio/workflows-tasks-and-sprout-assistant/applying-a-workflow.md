# Applying a Workflow

**How to add a workflow to a lead**

There are three ways to apply a workflow to a lead manually:

1. Go to the _Lead > Overview_ page, click the "Add" button to the right of the page, and click the "Apply Workflow" button. (This process is the same for leads and shoots)
2. Go to the _Lead > Tasks_ page and click the "Apply Workflow" button at the top of the page. (This process is the same for leads and shoots)
3. When booking a lead through a booking proposal (before it becomes a shoot), you can apply a shoot workflow once it is booked. Go to the _Lead > Booking Proposal_ page > Proposal Settings > and choose a workflow from the "Workflow" dropdown.

**How to add a workflow to a Lead automatically**

To **apply a workflow to a lead automatically,** go to Settings > Automation > Lead Workflows \*\*and click the "New Default Workflow" button. Choose which shoot type you'd like to automatically apply a workflow to, then choose which workflow you'd like to apply to that shoot type by default.

When you choose a default workflow for a shoot type, any new leads you create (through a contact form or by manually adding it) for that shoot type will automatically have that workflow applied to it.

## **How to add a workflow to a shoot**

There are three ways to apply a workflow to a shoot:

1. When adding a shoot via the "Add Shoot" modal, you can choose a workflow to apply to that shoot once it's created. Choose a workflow from the "Apply Workflow" dropdown on the last step of the "Add Shoot" modal.&#x20;
2. Go to the _Shoot > Overview_ page, click the "Quick Actions" button at the top of the page, and click the "Apply Workflow" button (Same as the lead explanation earlier)
3. Go to the _Shoot > Tasks_ page and click the "Apply Workflow" button at the top of the page. (Same as the lead explanation earlier)

## **If I change my template workflow, will it change all leads and shoots it's been applied to?**

Currently, a workflow is a "shortcut" to mass-apply a set of emails, questionnaires, tasks, etc. Updating a template workflow will not dynamically update all the leads and shoots it's been previously applied to.
